# Code of Conduct

Re:CV adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and welcoming, whatever someone's experience, background or identity. Harassment, insults,
personal attacks and publishing someone's private information are not accepted, in issues, pull requests, reviews or
any other project space.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour makes you uncomfortable, contact the maintainer privately at [thomas.m1104@protonmail.com](mailto:thomas.m1104@protonmail.com).
Reports are handled confidentially.

The maintainers may remove comments, commits or other contributions that do not follow this code, and may ban
temporarily or permanently anyone whose behaviour they consider harmful, as described in the Contributor Covenant's
enforcement guidelines.
